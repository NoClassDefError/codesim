1. Conventions

All components share the notation below. Every equation on this page is a direct transcription of getStateAndOutputEquationsSym, getPowerFlowEquations and initFromPowerFlow of the class; where the code simplifies algebraically, both forms are shown.

1.1 Units and time

  • Each device is written in per unit of its own base \((V_b, S_b)\) with current base \(I_b = S_b / (\sqrt{3/2}\,V_b)\). RLGrid rescales the port input and output when the device base differs from the system base.
  • Time is normalized: \(\tau = \omega_b t\) with \(\omega_b = 2\pi f\). Throughout, \(\dot x \equiv \mathrm{d}x/\mathrm{d}\tau\). Inductances \(L\) and capacitances \(C\) are therefore per-unit reactances and susceptances at nominal frequency, and a frequency \(\omega = 1\) is nominal.
  • Each symbol carries the device index as a suffix in the code (vCd_vsg); the index is omitted here.

1.2 Frames and operators

Port quantities are in the stationary \(\alpha\beta\) frame. A device with a frame angle \(\theta\) works internally in its own rotating \(dq\) frame. With

$$ \mathbf R(\theta)=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta & -\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta\end{bmatrix},\qquad \mathbf J=\begin{bmatrix}0 & -1\\ 1 & 0\end{bmatrix},\qquad \mathbf e(\phi)=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\phi\\ \sin\phi\end{bmatrix}, $$

a port vector is rotated in and out as \(\mathbf x_{dq}=\mathbf R(\theta)^{\top}\mathbf x_{\alpha\beta}\) and \(\mathbf x_{\alpha\beta}=\mathbf R(\theta)\,\mathbf x_{dq}\). \(\mathbf J\) is multiplication by \(j\): a term \(\omega L\mathbf J\mathbf i\) is the rotational (speed) voltage of an inductor in the \(dq\) frame, and \(-\omega\mathbf J\mathbf x\) in a state equation is the frame-rotation term. Bold symbols are 2-vectors \([x_d, x_q]^\top\), \(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=a_d b_d + a_q b_q\), and \(|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf a}\).

Frame angle and load angle. In the GFMs the frame angle obeys \(\dot\theta=\omega\) (the frequency reference), and the internal EMF sits at the angle \(\delta\) within that frame. The absolute EMF angle is \(\theta+\delta\) and its frequency is \(\omega+\dot\delta\). Frame angles have no initialization residual; they are set by the power flow.

1.3 Ports and power

Port typeInput \(\mathbf u\) (\(\alpha\beta\))Output \(\mathbf y\) (\(\alpha\beta\))
"Voltage"current \(\mathbf i\) the device delivers into the networkbus voltage \(\mathbf v\)
"Current"bus voltage \(\mathbf v\)current \(\mathbf i\) injected into the bus

Both port types use the generator convention: the power the device delivers to the bus is

$$ P = \mathbf v\cdot\mathbf i = v_d i_d + v_q i_q,\qquad Q = v_q i_d - v_d i_q . $$

A load therefore has negative \(P\). In the power flow, \(P\), \(Q\), \(V\) and \(\theta\) are the device's injection and its bus voltage magnitude and angle.

1.4 Limiter functions

The limiters are written with absolute values so that SymPy can differentiate them:

$$ \begin{aligned} \operatorname{pos}(x) &= \max(0,x) = \tfrac12\left(x+|x|\right),\\ \operatorname{clip}(x,a,b) &= a+\operatorname{pos}(x-a)-\operatorname{pos}(x-b),\\ \max(r,I) &= \sqrt{\tfrac12\left(r^2+I^2+|r^2-I^2|\right)}\quad(r,I\ge 0),\\ \operatorname{sat}_{I}(\mathbf i) &= \frac{I\,\mathbf i}{\max(|\mathbf i|, I)}\qquad\text{(circular limit: unchanged inside the circle, scaled onto it outside).} \end{aligned} $$

1.5 Power flow and initialization

Each device contributes two power-flow equations \(F(P,Q,V,\theta)=0\) at its bus (with its references substituted). After the power flow, initFromPowerFlow gives an initial guess of the device state in the frame of its bus voltage (\(\mathbf v_{dq}=[V,0]^\top\), so \(\theta\) equals the bus angle), and the system solves \(\mathbf f_{\text{init}}(\mathbf x)=\mathbf 0\) by least squares, where \(\mathbf f_{\text{init}}\) is every state equation except the frame angles (getFInit). In the steady state \(\mathbf i_{dq}\) at the terminal is \([P,-Q]^\top/V\).

RMS model. In simulateRMS the frame angles and the states listed in rmsDynamicStateNames stay differential and every other state becomes algebraic (\(0=f_i\)); the equations are written in a global frame rotating at 1 pu, so each frame-angle row becomes \(\dot\theta=\omega-1\).

Flux–current relation

$$ \mathbf X_d=\begin{bmatrix}X_l+X_{ad} & X_{ad} & X_{ad}\\ X_{ad} & X_{1d}+X_{ad} & X_{ad}+X_{f1d}\\ X_{ad} & X_{ad}+X_{f1d} & X_{fd}+X_{ad}\end{bmatrix},\qquad \mathbf X_q=\begin{bmatrix}X_l+X_{aq} & X_{aq}\\ X_{aq} & X_{aq}+X_{1q}\end{bmatrix}, $$
$$ \begin{bmatrix}i'_d\\ i_{kd}\\ i_{fd}\end{bmatrix}=\mathbf X_d^{-1}\begin{bmatrix}\psi_d\\ \psi_{kd}\\ \psi_{fd}\end{bmatrix},\qquad \begin{bmatrix}i'_q\\ i_{kq}\end{bmatrix}=\mathbf X_q^{-1}\begin{bmatrix}\psi_q\\ \psi_{kq}\end{bmatrix},\qquad i_d=-i'_d,\quad i_q=-i'_q . $$

The inverses are stored as parameters Gd11…Gd33 and Gq11…Gq22 and are recomputed on a base change. \(i_d, i_q\) are the stator currents injected into the bus.

Equations

With \(\mathbf v=\mathbf R(\theta)^{\top}\mathbf v_{\alpha\beta}\), \(P=\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf i\), \(Q=v_q i_d-v_d i_q\):

$$ \begin{aligned} \dot\psi_d &= v_d+R_s i_d+\omega\psi_q, & \dot\psi_q &= v_q+R_s i_q-\omega\psi_d,\\ \dot\psi_{kd} &= -R_{1d}\,i_{kd}, & \dot\psi_{kq} &= -R_{1q}\,i_{kq},\\ \dot\psi_{fd} &= V_{f0}+v_r-R_{fd}\,i_{fd}, & \dot v_r &= \frac{-v_r+K_a\left(V_{d,ref}-|\mathbf v|-K_qQ\right)}{T_a},\\ \dot\theta &= \omega, & \dot\omega &= \frac{P_{ref}-P-(D+K_g)(\omega-\omega_{ref})}{M}, \end{aligned} $$
$$ \mathbf i_{\alpha\beta}=\mathbf R(\theta)\,[i_d,\;i_q]^\top . $$

Power flow and initialization

$$ P-P_{ref}=0,\qquad V+K_qQ-\left(V_{d,ref}^2+V_{q,ref}^2\right)=0 . $$

The initial guess solves the classical phasor diagram with \(X_q=X_l+X_{aq}\): of the two candidate load angles it keeps the one with the larger field current, then builds the fluxes and sets \(v_r=R_{fd}i_{fd}-V_{f0}\), \(\theta=\theta_{bus}-\delta\), \(\omega=\omega_0\). Every equation except \(\dot\theta\) is an initialization residual. (SMIB__SG uses its own initialization; see the system reference.)

4. Grid-forming converters

All GFMs share the synchronization law: a frame rotating at the frequency reference (\(\dot\theta=\omega\), \(\omega\equiv\omega_{ref}\)) and an internal EMF \(\mathbf E=V_{ref}\,\mathbf e(\delta)\) whose angle integrates the active-power error,

$$ \dot\delta = K_p\left(P_{ref}-P_{meas}\right), $$

so \(K_p\) is a P–f droop gain (frequency deviation \(\dot\delta\) per unit power error). What differs is where \(P_{meas}\) is measured, how the EMF is turned into a current, and how the current is limited. In the RMS model \(\delta\) stays differential.

GFM_A_C_LC

Voltage portcomponents/gfm_a_c_lc.py

Virtual-admittance GFM: the EMF drives a virtual \(R_v+jL_v\) branch whose current is the reference of an inner PI current loop on the filter inductor \(L_f\); the reference is limited to a circle of radius \(I_{max}\). The terminal capacitor \(C\) is part of the device, so the output is its voltage.

Equations

\(\mathbf i_g=\mathbf R(\theta)^{\top}\mathbf i_{\alpha\beta}\) is the current delivered to the network.

$$ \begin{aligned} L_v\,\dot{\mathbf i}_{ref} &= \mathbf E-\mathbf v_C-R_v\,\mathbf i_{ref}-\omega L_v\mathbf J\,\mathbf i_{ref} &&\text{virtual admittance}\\ \boldsymbol\varepsilon &= \operatorname{sat}_{I_{max}}(\mathbf i_{ref})-\mathbf i_L,\qquad \dot{\mathbf x}_{cc}=\boldsymbol\varepsilon &&\text{limited current error}\\ \mathbf v_{br} &= \tfrac12\mathbf v_C+\omega L_f\mathbf J\,\mathbf i_L+K_{p,cc}\boldsymbol\varepsilon+K_{i,cc}\mathbf x_{cc} &&\text{bridge voltage}\\ L_f\,\dot{\mathbf i}_L &= \mathbf v_{br}-\mathbf v_C-\omega L_f\mathbf J\,\mathbf i_L = -\tfrac12\mathbf v_C+K_{p,cc}\boldsymbol\varepsilon+K_{i,cc}\mathbf x_{cc}\\ C\,\dot{\mathbf v}_C &= \mathbf i_L-\mathbf i_g-\omega C\mathbf J\,\mathbf v_C\\ \dot\delta &= K_p\left(P_{ref}-\mathbf v_C\cdot\mathbf i_L\right),\qquad \dot\theta=\omega,\qquad \mathbf v_{\alpha\beta}=\mathbf R(\theta)\,\mathbf v_C . \end{aligned} $$

The bridge feeds forward half of the capacitor voltage and decouples the inductor's speed voltage; the current integrator supplies the other half in the steady state (\(\mathbf x_{cc}=\mathbf v_C/(2K_{i,cc})\)). The power is measured at the capacitor with the filter current.

Power flow and initialization

$$ \mathbf i_L=\frac{[P,-Q]^\top}{V}+\omega C\mathbf J\,[V,0]^\top,\quad \mathbf E_{ss}=[V,0]^\top+\left(R_v\mathbf I+\omega L_v\mathbf J\right)\mathbf i_L;\qquad P_{ref}-P=0,\quad |\mathbf E_{ss}|^2-V_{ref}^2=0 . $$

Initial state: \(\mathbf v_C=[V,0]^\top\), \(\mathbf i_{ref}=\mathbf i_L\), \(\mathbf x_{cc}=\mathbf v_C/(2K_{i,cc})\), \(\delta=\angle\mathbf E_{ss}\), \(\theta\) = bus angle.

Power flow and initialization

$$ \mathbf i=\frac{[P,-Q]^\top}{V},\; \mathbf v_{pcc}=[V,0]^\top+(R_g\mathbf I+\omega L_g\mathbf J)\,\mathbf i,\; \mathbf E_{ss}=\mathbf v_{pcc}+(R_v\mathbf I+\omega L_v\mathbf J)\,\mathbf i;\quad P_{ref}-\mathbf v_{pcc}\cdot\mathbf i=0,\;|\mathbf E_{ss}|^2-V_{ref}^2=0 . $$

Note that \(P_{ref}\) is the power at the converter side of the embedded branch, not at the port bus. Initial state: \(\mathbf i_{ref}=\mathbf i\), \(\mathbf x_{cc}=\mathbf v_{pcc}/(2K_{i,cc})\), \(\delta=\angle\mathbf E_{ss}\).

5. Grid-following converters

5.1 Shared blocks: PLL, current loop and FRT priority

The GFL works in the PLL frame (\(\theta\) is the PLL angle) with measured voltage \(\mathbf v\).

PLL with frequency limit and anti-windup

$$ \omega_{raw}=\omega_0+K_{p,PLL}\,v_q+K_{i,PLL}\,x_{PLL},\qquad \omega=\operatorname{clip}(\omega_{raw},\omega_{min},\omega_{max}),\qquad \dot x_{PLL}=v_q+K_{aw}\left(\omega-\omega_{raw}\right),\qquad\dot\theta=\omega . $$

Current priority (normal and FRT/LVRT)

In normal operation the active current has priority and the reactive current takes what is left of the circle \(I_{max}\). Below the voltage threshold \(V_{th}\) the reactive support current \(-K_1(V_{th}-|\mathbf v|)\) has priority and the active current is capped at \(I_{d2}\). The two are blended by a steep logistic switch:

$$ \begin{aligned} \text{normal: }& i_d^{N}=\operatorname{clip}(i_d^{0},0,I_{max}), & i_q^{N}&=\operatorname{clip}\!\left(i_q^{0},-\ell_q,\ell_q\right),\;\ell_q=\sqrt{\operatorname{pos}\!\left(I_{max}^2-(i_d^{N})^2\right)},\\ \text{FRT: }& i_q^{F}=\operatorname{clip}\!\left(-K_1(V_{th}-|\mathbf v|),-I_{max},I_{max}\right), & i_d^{F}&=\min\!\left(I_{d2},\ell_d\right),\;\ell_d=\sqrt{\operatorname{pos}\!\left(I_{max}^2-(i_q^{F})^2\right)},\\ &m=\tfrac12\left(1+\tanh\!\left(50\,(V_{th}-|\mathbf v|)\right)\right), & \mathbf i^{\star}&=(1-m)\,\mathbf i^{N}+m\,\mathbf i^{F}. \end{aligned} $$

A negative \(i_q\) injects reactive power (\(Q=-v_d i_q\) when \(v_q=0\)). In GFL_C_F_L \(i_d^F\) is additionally clipped at 0 from below.

Inner current loop

The bridge voltage is \(\mathbf v_{conv}=\mathbf v+\omega L\mathbf J\mathbf i+K_{p,cc}\boldsymbol\varepsilon+K_{i,cc}\mathbf x_{cc}\) with \(\boldsymbol\varepsilon=\mathbf i^{\star}-\mathbf i\), so the filter equation reduces to a pure PI-driven integrator:

$$ L\,\dot{\mathbf i}=\mathbf v_{conv}-\mathbf v-\omega L\mathbf J\,\mathbf i=K_{p,cc}\,\boldsymbol\varepsilon+K_{i,cc}\,\mathbf x_{cc},\qquad \dot{\mathbf x}_{cc}=\boldsymbol\varepsilon,\qquad \mathbf i_{\alpha\beta}=\mathbf R(\theta)\,\mathbf i . $$
