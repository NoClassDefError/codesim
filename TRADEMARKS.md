to use the CodeSim name, logos, service marks, or other project identifiers as

not claim rights beyond those available under applicable law.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- truthfully state that software is based on, derived from, compatible with, or
  uses CodeSim;
- link to the official CodeSim project;
- reproduce the CodeSim name where required by `LICENSE` or `NOTICE`;
- redistribute an unmodified official release using its original name, while
  preserving all required notices; and
- use the name in commentary, research, teaching, reviews, and other
  descriptive references that do not suggest sponsorship or endorsement.

Examples include "based on CodeSim" and "compatible with CodeSim" when those
statements are accurate.

## Uses that require prior written permission

Prior written permission is required to:

- name a modified distribution or product `CodeSim` or use a confusingly
  similar product name;
- use a CodeSim logo as the primary branding for a modified distribution,
  product, company, service, domain name, package, or marketplace listing;
- describe a product or service as "official," "certified," "approved," or
  "endorsed" by CodeSim; or
- use CodeSim branding in a way that is likely to imply sponsorship,
  affiliation, or responsibility by the official project.

Modified distributions should use a distinct name and logo, state that they
are modified, identify the upstream CodeSim version where practical, and avoid
confusion with official releases.

## Community groups and events

Community groups, workshops, and events may use the CodeSim name descriptively,
such as "CodeSim users group," if they clearly state that they are independent
and do not imply official sponsorship. Use of a CodeSim logo still requires
permission unless supplied expressly for that purpose.

## Permission requests

For a non-confidential trademark or branding request, open an issue in the
official CodeSim repository and label it as a trademark request. Do not include
confidential business information in a public issue.
